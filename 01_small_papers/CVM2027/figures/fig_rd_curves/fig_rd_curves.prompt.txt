================================================================

- Palette: replace rainbow tab10 with a **perceptually ordered, colourblind-safe** scheme.
  Recommended: use viridis/cividis sampled across the 7 sequences (ordered by rate or by name),
  OR a curated 7-colour subset that avoids red-green pairs (e.g. blue→green→orange family:
  #0072B2, #56B4E9, #009E73, #E69F00, #D55E00, #CC79A7, #000000). Distinguish sequences ALSO
  by marker shape (o s ^ v D p *) so hue is not the only cue.
- baseline = solid + filled circle; final = dashed + filled square; both markers 3 pt.
- IQR band: same colour as its line, alpha 0.15–0.20, no edge.
- Axes: x "rate (bytes/primitive)", y "PSNR (dB)" / "static-region PSNR (dB)", Arial 7 pt,
  ticks 6 pt, direction in, thin spines, light grid alpha 0.25.
- Remove in-panel titles (let the LaTeX caption say full-image vs static). If a distinction is
  needed, put small bold "(a)"/"(b)" top-left, 8 pt.
- Legend: frameless, 6 pt, ≤2 columns, moved OUTSIDE crowding (best empty corner or just below).
- White background; output vector PDF, double-column width 183 mm.
