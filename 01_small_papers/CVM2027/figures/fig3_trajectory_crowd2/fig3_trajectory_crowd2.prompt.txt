================================================================
中文意图说明
================================================================
估计轨迹 vs 真值的 2D 俯视对比（crowd2）。3 条线：base 臂、gate 臂、ground truth；
两条估计臂均已 SE(3) 对齐到 GT 的世界帧。x 轴 z(m)、y 轴 x(m)，等比例。

待改进点：
1. 默认 matplotlib 配色/线型偏"AI 味"，不够出版级
2. 图例位置可能压线；坐标轴 tick 偏密
3. 缺网格轻重层次；两条臂与 GT 的区分靠颜色（色盲不友好）

================================================================
Restyle spec（绘图代码重画，数据不变）
================================================================
Keep the DATA identical (same arrays, same SE(3) alignment). Redraw only the STYLE.

- Lines: ground truth = black dotted (#000000), base = blue solid (#0072B2),
  gate = vermillion dashed (#D55E00). Colourblind-safe; distinguish ALSO by linestyle
  (solid vs dashed vs dotted). Linewidth 1.0–1.2 pt.
- Axes: "x (m)" and "z (m)", Arial 7 pt; equal aspect; ticks 6 pt, few (sparse),
  direction in, thin 0.5 pt spine.
- Grid: very light (alpha 0.25), behind data.
- Legend: frameless (frameon=False), 6–7 pt, placed in an empty corner, no box.
- No title. No chartjunk. White background.
- Output: vector PDF, single-column width 89 mm.

================================================================
Negative / do-not
================================================================
Do NOT invent, smooth, resample, or "prettify" the trajectory shape. Data points fixed.
Do NOT add 3D view, no arrows, no annotation balloons unless in the LaTeX caption.
