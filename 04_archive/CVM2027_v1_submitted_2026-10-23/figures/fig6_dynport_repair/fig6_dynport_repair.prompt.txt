中文意图说明

"离线修复"定性对比：4 行 × 3 列照片。
- 4 行 = 2 序列 × 2 帧（crowd2 f271 / crowd2 f621 / balloon2 f146 / balloon2 f321）
- 3 列 = Ground truth | Erased (base map) | Repaired (offline)
- 左列（GT）用暖色方框圈出"动态区"；中、右列各有一个暖色放大插框，框住同一动态区，
  直观展示：抹除列缺人 → 修复列把人补回
- 行左标签：序列名 + 帧号

================================================================
Style-restructure spec（给绘图工具/人工，或图像编辑模型，只动排版）
================================================================
Goal: re-compose the EXISTING rendered photographs into a publication-grade 4×3 montage.
ABSOLUTELY DO NOT alter/beautify/denoise/inpaint/regenerate any photo pixel — evidence only.
Allowed: lossless crop, uniform scale, alignment, re-badging of frames/labels.
NOTE: the "Repaired" column legitimately shows a blurred reconstruction — that blur is the
TRUE result; do not sharpen or clean it.

Grid: 4 rows × 3 columns. Columns (top headers, 7 pt): "Ground truth", "Erased (base map)",
"Repaired (offline)". Row labels far-left (two lines: sequence then frame), Arial 7 pt,
right-aligned: "crowd2 / f271", "crowd2 / f621", "balloon2 / f146", "balloon2 / f321".

Dynamic-region rectangle: in the LEFT column of each row, draw a thin rectangle marking the
region of interest (vermillion #D55E00, 1.0 pt). In the MIDDLE and RIGHT columns of the same
row, draw one zoom inset (lower-right) showing exactly that same region magnified; draw the
same rectangle as the inset's border. The correspondence GT-box ↔ insets must be visually
obvious (same colour, same aspect).

Typography: Arial/Helvetica 6–7 pt throughout; column headers 7 pt; row labels 7 pt.
Uniform row heights; uniform column widths; small even gaps (~3 pt). White background.
Optional (a)(b)(c)(d) row panel letters, 8 pt bold, top-left of each row.
Colourblind-safe accent only (vermillion #D55E00); photos untouched.
Output vector container + ≥300 dpi images; canvas ≈ 1.24:1 (183 mm wide); no title, no border.

================================================================
Negative / do-not
================================================================
Do NOT regenerate, sharpen, or "fix" the repaired (blurred) photos — that blur is the result.
Do NOT add icons/arrows/captions inside. Rows/cols fixed at 4×3. Do not change frame selection.
