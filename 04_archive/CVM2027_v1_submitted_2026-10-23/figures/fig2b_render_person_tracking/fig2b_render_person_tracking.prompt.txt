================================================================
Style-restructure spec（给绘图工具/人工，或图像编辑模型，只动排版）
================================================================
Goal: re-compose the EXISTING person_tracking photographs into a publication-grade montage,
IDENTICAL in layout to fig2_render_crowd2 so the two sequences read as one family.
ABSOLUTELY DO NOT alter/beautify/denoise/regenerate photo pixels — evidence only.
Allowed: lossless crop, uniform scale, alignment, re-badging, frames.

Grid: 3 rows × 3 columns. Rows: "Ground truth" / "base" / "gate". Columns = 3 fixed frames.
Row labels far-left, right-aligned, 7 pt, same backing band style as fig2.
Per-photo inset zoom (rows 2–3): lower-right, zooms the vacated region; one accent colour
(vermillion #D55E00) 0.75 pt; identical size and corner across the grid and across fig2.
Column frame index under each column, Arial 6 pt grey. Optional (a)(b)(c) top-left, 8 pt bold.
Font Arial/Helvetica 6–7 pt; uniform gaps ~2 pt; white background; colourblind-safe.
Output vector container + ≥300 dpi images; canvas ≈ 1.66:1 (183 mm wide); no title/border.

================================================================
Negative / do-not
================================================================
Do NOT regenerate or "improve" any photo. Do NOT differ from fig2's layout (consistency is
the requirement). No icons/arrows/captions. Rows/cols fixed at 3×3.
